# Code of conduct

INKPLAY adopts the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. The full text is at that link and applies to
everyone in this project's spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, and
the gallery and arena on the live site.

In short: be welcoming and kind, assume good intent, give and take
feedback gracefully, and keep it about the work. Harassment, slurs,
sexualised content, personal attacks and publishing other people's private
information are not okay, and that includes what you draw for the gallery.

## Reporting

If someone is behaving badly, report it privately to the maintainer,
[@dhananjay-123](https://github.com/dhananjay-123), through a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/dhananjay-123/inkplay/security/advisories/new)
(it reaches only the maintainers) or GitHub's
[report abuse](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
tools. Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may remove comments, code, levels or other content that breaks
this code of conduct, and may ban people who repeatedly or seriously break
it, following the enforcement guidelines in the Contributor Covenant.
